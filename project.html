Reading List

Here are six titles I actively read:

Batman (2025)

The brand new ongoing launched in 2025. Written by Matt Faction and illustrated by Jorge Jimenez.

Spawn (1992)

The 34 year long ongoing series. Written by Todd McFarlane and illustrated by various artists.

Superman (2023)

The 2023 relaunch of the iconic superhero series. Written by Joshua Williamson and illustrated by Dan Mora.

Absolute Wonder Woman (2024)

The Dark reboot of Wonder Woman. Written by Kelly Thompson and illustrated by Hayden Sherman.

Green Lantern (2023)

The 2023 relaunch of the iconic superhero. Written by Jeremy Adams and illustrated by Xermanico.

Moon Knight (2021)

The 2021 series written by Jed Mackay and illustrated by various artists, most notably by Alessandrio Cappucio.
